Put the game rules on the page

The hosted page announces an event. Turn that same page into the rules of rock-paper-scissors. Still no JavaScript. Commit the change with the loop from the previous chapter.

A player needs the winning pairs before the first round.

Inputs
The three winning pairs and the tie rule.
Expected output
A heading, one instruction, the picture, and a list of the three pairs.
Rules
Keep the card styles. Do not invent a fourth move.
public/index.html
<!doctype html>
<html lang="en">
<head>
  <meta charset="utf-8">
  <meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1">
  <title>Rock · Paper · Scissors</title>
  <link rel="stylesheet" href="style.css">
  
</head>
<body>
  <main class="card">
    <h1>Rock · Paper · Scissors</h1>
    <p>Choose one move each round. Identical moves tie.</p>
    <img src="mark.svg" alt="The letters RPS on a dark card" width="160">
    <ul>
      <li>Rock beats scissors.</li>
      <li>Paper beats rock.</li>
      <li>Scissors beats paper.</li>
    </ul>
  </main>
</body>
</html>
